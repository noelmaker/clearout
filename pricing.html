PRICING

Clear pricing, without the guesswork.

Junk removal projects can vary quite a bit. Understanding what affects the cost can help you know what to expect before scheduling.

01

How much needs to go?

The amount of material being removed is one of the biggest factors in determining the scope of a junk removal project. A few individual items are very different from a full property cleanout.

02

What types of items?

Furniture, appliances, yard debris, construction materials, and household clutter can all require different handling. The types of items involved help determine how the project should be approached.

03

Where are the items?

Location matters too. Items that are easy to access may be simpler to remove than material stored in an attic, basement, garage, or another difficult-to-reach area.

04

What does the project involve?

A straightforward pickup and a larger cleanout can have very different scopes. Providing clear project details helps create a more accurate understanding of the work involved.
